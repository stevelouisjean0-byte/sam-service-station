Questions people actually have

Can I eat there, or is it takeaway?

Not published. Nothing on the listing says whether there is seating. Reviewers describe the food, not the room. Worth asking on the call.

Do they take card?

Not published. No payment methods appear on the listing. Carry cash as a fallback and ask when you ring.

Is the kitchen open the whole time the shop is?

Unknown, and worth checking. The 8 AM to 12 AM hours are published for the business as a whole. A kitchen inside a shop often runs shorter hours than the shop, so if you are going specifically for food, ring first.

Is there a website or online ordering?

No. The Google listing has no website attached, and no ordering link. The phone is the only channel.
