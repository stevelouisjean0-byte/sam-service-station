Read this before you scroll. Sam Service Station publishes no menu, no dish list and no prices — not on Google, not anywhere. This page therefore contains no menu. Everything below is quoted or counted from customer reviews, and each item says which review it came from.

A made-up menu would be the single most damaging thing this site could do. Turning up for something that was never on the stove is worse than turning up not knowing.

How to order, given all that

  1. 1 · Ring during opening hours

    8 AM to 12 AM any day. (407) 290-2380. The badge above tells you if that is right now.

  2. 2 · Ask what is cooked today

    A kitchen this size cooks a changing plate. This is the question that actually gets you an answer, and it costs one minute.

  3. 3 · Ask about the price and the portion

    No prices are published. What customers volunteer is that the amount of food is generous for what it costs — but nobody has published a number, so ask.
